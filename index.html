How It Works

Two stages work together: one chooses the station, the other turns it back into something usable.


1 · The Tank Circuit

The coil (L) and tuning capacitor (C) form a parallel LC circuit. Energy sloshes between the two at one natural frequency; signals near it are strongly selected, others are rejected. Turning the capacitor changes C, which moves the station.

f₀ = 1 / (2π√(LC))

2 · The Detector

The diode lets current through only on positive peaks, charging the capacitor up to the peak. The resistor lets it discharge slowly, so the output follows the signal's envelope — the shape that carries the sound.

τ = R·C

InputDetector output

While the input peaks exceed the output, the diode conducts and the capacitor charges. Between peaks the diode is off and the capacitor drains through R. The diode drop is an assumed value (not a measured 1N60P figure), and source resistance is ignored.
